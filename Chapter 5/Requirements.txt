# Functional and Non-Functional Requirements

## Functional Requirements

1. **User Registration and Login Portal**
   - The system shall provide a webpage for user registration and login.
   - The system shall allow users to register by providing their name, email, phone number, and a profile picture.
   - The system shall allow users to log in using their email and password.

2. **User Data Management**
   - The system shall store user names, emails, phone numbers, and profile pictures.
   - The system shall allow users to update their profile information, including name, email, phone number, and profile picture.

3. **Password Management**
   - The system shall provide a secure password creation mechanism.
   - The system shall authenticate users using their email and password.

4. **User Profile Viewing and Editing**
   - The system shall allow users to view other user profiles.
   - The system shall allow users to edit their own profile details.

## Non-Functional Requirements

1. **Security**
   - The system shall use encryption to store passwords securely.
   - The system shall implement measures to protect against common security threats such as SQL injection, cross-site scripting (XSS), and cross-site request forgery (CSRF).

2. **Performance**
   - The system shall handle up to 1000 concurrent users without performance degradation.
   - The system shall load the user profile page within 2 seconds under normal load conditions.

3. **Usability**
   - The system shall have an intuitive and user-friendly interface.
   - The system shall provide clear error messages and guidance for user actions.

4. **Scalability**
   - The system shall be designed to scale horizontally to accommodate increasing numbers of users.
   - The system shall support the addition of new features with minimal impact on existing functionality.

5. **Availability**
   - The system shall have an uptime of 99.9% to ensure high availability.
   - The system shall provide mechanisms for backup and recovery to prevent data loss.

6. **Compliance**
   - The system shall comply with relevant data protection and privacy regulations, such as GDPR.
   - The system shall ensure that user data is handled in accordance with legal requirements.

7. **Maintainability**
   - The system shall be designed with modularity to facilitate easy maintenance and updates.
   - The system shall include comprehensive documentation for developers and administrators.

8. **Compatibility**
   - The system shall be compatible with major web browsers (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
   - The system shall support both desktop and mobile devices.
